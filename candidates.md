## Judge criteria (from the kit brief, in the brief's own order)

The judge is briefed as a comedy club booker who has heard every joke twice.
Criteria, most important first:

1. **C1 — Funny & instantly gettable:** a funny one-line joke a stranger
   would get in one read.
2. **C2 — Surprises:** sets up an expectation, then breaks it.
3. **C3 — Tight:** every word earns its place; the laugh lands at the end.
4. **C4 — Fresh:** not a stock joke, not a heard-it pun, not predictable
   from its first half.
5. **C5 — Reads cleanly:** clean aloud, no muddled wording, no reference
   that needs context.

Pairwise rule from the brief: go through the criteria in order, let the
first one where two lines differ decide, unless a later one differs by
much more. Every candidate below is scored 0–5 on C1–C5 in that order
(total /25 is only a seeding aid; the knockout follows the ordered rule).
